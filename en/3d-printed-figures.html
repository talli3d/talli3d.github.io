Questions and answers

Can you make a figure from a picture?

Yes. Send a clear picture, from the front and side if you can. We tell you what is possible, then send the quote.

Are cake toppers safe to put on a cake?

Toppers are decorations: place them on top of the cake or on a small base, and remove them before cutting and eating.

How small can the details be?

Fine detail prints best in PLA. Very thin parts may be made a little thicker so the figure does not break; we tell you before we print.

How much does it cost?

Every piece is priced on its own, by size, detail and colours. You send your idea and we reply on WhatsApp with a clear quote in AED before we start. Nothing is printed until you approve it.

Do you deliver across the UAE?

Yes. We print in the UAE and deliver to every emirate, including Fujairah, Kalba, Khor Fakkan, Sharjah, Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Umm Al Quwain and Al Ain. Pickup is also possible.

Which materials do you use?

PLA for fine detail and smooth colours, and PETG when the piece needs to be stronger or handle heat, for example in a car.

How do I order?

Open the order page, pick the type, add pictures, size and colours, and send. We reply on WhatsApp with the quote, you approve each item with one tap, and we print and deliver.
